## 🚀 Featured projects

**📦 Multi-warehouse inventory management (final-year project)**
Hybrid SARIMAX + XGBoost demand forecasting with Ramadan/Eid regressors, combined with ILP and genetic-algorithm stock optimization across 84 weekly time series (21 product categories × 4 regions). Integrated into a production WMS platform (WASL) during my internship at Ourquilane.
`Python` `SARIMAX` `XGBoost` `OR / ILP` `Genetic algorithms`
→ [Repository](https://github.com/MahdiTir/pfe_ecommerce_algeria)

**📡 Djezzy Data Nexus (DSIP4, Djezzy × MajestEYE)**
Full-stack decision-support platform for telecom infrastructure operations: site monitoring, digital-twin views, incident diagnosis, ticket workflows and maintenance planning, built on messy SCADA data. Includes environmental fault prediction for base stations (AC units, generators).
`Python` `Machine learning` `Predictive maintenance` `Full-stack`
→ [Repository](https://github.com/mounir19000/djezzy-data-nexus)

**🌿 EDEN (Samsung Innovation Campus)**
Offline-first mobile app with on-device computer vision, multilingual (AR / FR / EN) and gamified.
`TensorFlow Lite` `Android` `Offline-first`

**🦯 IRCHAD**
Indoor navigation assistant for visually impaired users: MVVM Android app, Raspberry Pi communication over sockets, Room database, TalkBack accessibility.
`Kotlin` `MVVM` `Room` `Raspberry Pi`

**🩺 Medical appointment app**

`Django REST` `Supabase` `Mobile`
